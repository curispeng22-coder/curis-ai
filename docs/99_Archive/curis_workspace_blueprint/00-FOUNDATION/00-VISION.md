# Vision

Status: Deprecated

Curis Workspace is not another AI chat tool. It is a Personal Business OS for solo entrepreneurs.

The long-term vision is to let one person operate like a small company by managing business scopes, departments, AI employees, workflows, knowledge, tools, models, and external platforms in one unified system.

## Core Statement

Curis Workspace helps every solo entrepreneur own and operate an AI-powered company at the lowest practical cost.

## What It Replaces

It does not replace ChatGPT, GitHub, Obsidian, CapCut, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat Official Account, brokerage platforms, e-commerce platforms, or office tools.

It replaces the inefficient way one person manually switches between many roles, many tools, many models, and many workflows without a unified operating system.
