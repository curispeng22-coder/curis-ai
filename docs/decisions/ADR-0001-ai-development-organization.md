# ADR-0001: Establish AI Development Organization Before Business Development

## Status

Draft for review.

## Context

Curis Workspace is starting from an empty repository. Before building business functionality, the project needs an engineering organization that defines how AI agents and human collaborators will work together.

## Decision

Create an AI Development Organization baseline before Sprint 1. This baseline defines team roles, responsibility boundaries, collaboration protocol, directory rules, documentation rules, coding rules, and Sprint workflow.

## Consequences

Positive:

- Business development starts with clear ownership and gates.
- Future agents have a source of truth for process and standards.
- Sprint 1 scope can be reviewed against explicit standards.

Trade-offs:

- Business implementation is delayed until review approval.
- Standards may need refinement after the first real Sprint.

## Approval Gate

Sprint 1 cannot begin until `docs/sprints/sprint-0-organization-review.md` is approved.
